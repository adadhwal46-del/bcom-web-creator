# First-year BCom student portfolio

## What to build
- Four linked pages: **Home**, **About**, **Services**, and **Contact**, with a shared navigation bar and footer.
- Home introduces sample student **Alex Morgan**, a first-year BCom student, with a short profile, academic focus, and links to capabilities and contact.
- About presents sample education, interests, and career goals without invented achievements.
- Services presents the five requested student capabilities with honest beginner-level descriptions.
- Contact shows a clearly marked sample email address and an editable message form. The form prepares an email in the visitor’s email app; it does not claim to send or store messages.
- Clearly label sample identity, college, and contact information so they can be replaced before publishing.

## Appearance
- Academic-professional design: white background, ink-colored text, restrained blue accents, spacious full-width sections, and fine dividers.
- Readable **Manrope** typography, modest rounded corners, subtle transitions, and visible keyboard focus.
- A relevant study-desk image, paired with a prominent student introduction. Mobile navigation collapses into an accessible menu.

## Technical details
- Use existing TanStack routes and shared design-system controls, with semantic colors in global CSS.
- Give each page distinct titles and social descriptions.
- Keep this informational portfolio frontend-only; no accounts or message storage are required.

## Verification
- Test navigation across all four pages, mobile menu, form validation, and email preparation with sample input.
- Check desktop and mobile layouts for clipping and overlap, verify the study image loads, and inspect errors before reporting completion.
